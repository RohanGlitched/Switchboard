A food pantry's hardest job is the inbox.

Riverside Community Pantry feeds 400 households a week with one paid Coordinator. About 150 messages land every week. Most already have an answer, written down in the handbook she wrote herself. A few do not — and she reaches those last, tired. Switchboard reads all of them, handles what it has been trusted with, and stops at everything else.

What it handled

Acted alone, on the record

  • Searched the handbook before answering, every time
  • Booked and cancelled shifts, warning when Saturday drops below four
  • Registered households and checked the service ZIPs
  • Recorded donations and sent the reply
  • Wrote every action to a ledger the Coordinator can read back

The Desk

Stopped, because it needed a person

  • Why it stopped — stated from policy, not guessed by the model
  • What it would do, and two or three real alternatives
  • A drafted reply, ready to send or edit
  • The handbook clauses it relied on, quoted for checking
  • Approve, edit, or take it yourself — in one click

The idea: autonomy is a dial, not a switch

Auto

It acts alone and tells you afterwards.

Hours & location · Shift signup · Shift cancellation · Household intake · Partner referral · Shelf-stable donation
Ask

It researches, drafts, recommends — then stops. You decide.

Dietary substitution · Past best-by date · Perishable donation · Home-made food · Bulk or corporate donation
Never

It will never act alone here, however well it performs.

Volunteer under 18 · Severe allergy · Household in crisis · Complaint or incident · Press or public official

It earns the next tier

An Ask class moves to Auto only after you approve its recommendation three times without changing a word. Then it asks you for the promotion itself.

An edit resets the count to zero

Not pauses — resets. If you had to fix the answer, it had not learned the thing yet. And no number of approvals unlocks a Never class: the promotion path does not exist, on disk or in memory.

The rule is a control in the execution path, not a line in a prompt

def _before_tool(self, event):
    name = _tool_name(event)
    if name in READ_ONLY:
        return

    cls = current_class()
    if not cls:
        self._cancel(event, name, "no class established")
        return

    if not POLICY.may_act_alone(cls):
        self._cancel(event, name, "requires a human")

A Strands BeforeToolCallEvent hook. Every write — booking, cancelling, registering, referring, recording, replying — passes through it. Ask the model nicely to send the reply anyway and the call is still cancelled, before any state changes.
